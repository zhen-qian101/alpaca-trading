## Face models (OpenCV Zoo)

Both models are used only by the recap's tooling, through OpenCV's
`FaceDetectorYN` and `FaceRecognizerSF`: `briefing/host_identity_score.py`
(the identity check on the stylist's looks) and `briefing/h3_lipsync_score.py`
(mouth-movement scoring).

- **YuNet,** `briefing/assets/models/yunet.onnx`. Byte-identical to OpenCV
  Zoo's `face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx` (SHA-256
  `8f2383e4dd3cfbb4553ea8718107fc0423210dc964f9f4280604804ed2552fa4`).
  Source: <https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet>,
  mirrored at <https://huggingface.co/opencv/face_detection_yunet>.
  Licence: MIT, "Copyright (c) 2020 Shiqi Yu" (text below; the upstream
  licence file also gives the author's contact address).
- **SFace,** `briefing/assets/models/sface.onnx`. Byte-identical to OpenCV
  Zoo's `face_recognition_sface_2021dec.onnx` (SHA-256
  `0ba9fbfa01b5270c96627c4ef784da859931e02f04419c829e83484087c34e79`), a
  conversion from MXNet of the model in "SFace: Sigmoid-Constrained
  Hypersphere Loss for Robust Face Recognition" (Zhong et al.,
  arXiv:2205.12010). Source:
  <https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_recognition_sface>,
  mirrored at <https://huggingface.co/opencv/face_recognition_sface>.
  Licence: Apache License 2.0 (text below). The upstream licence names no
  copyright holder, and the model's directory has no NOTICE file.

## Credited, not copied

The two live strategies re-implement, in Python, the trading rules of two
public TradingView scripts by ChartArt: "MACD + SMA 200 Strategy"
(`backtest/macd_sma.py`) and "RSI + Bollinger Bands, Double Strategy" v1.1
(`backtest/bollinger_rsi.py`). The repository holds no copy of the original
Pine Script source, and the modules change the rules (long-only, decisions at
bar close, an added stop), as their docstrings describe. The terms the
original scripts were published under have not been verified.
